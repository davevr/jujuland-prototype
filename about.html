About

↑ not actually me — a portrait goes here

Hi! I draw. Mostly creatures, mostly at night, mostly when I should be doing homework. I've been drawing since I could hold a crayon (evidence in the Attic) and on an iPad since age twelve.

I love the moment a drawing starts glowing — when the light shows up and the picture stops being lines. If you like what you see, the best things you can do are: use a wallpaper, tell a friend, or say hi.

Things I will draw forever: foxes, dragons, whales, libraries, weather.

Juju is a digital artist working primarily in Procreate and Photoshop. Her practice centers on luminous, story-driven imagery — creatures and places at the border of the everyday and the imagined.

Working from life, from sketchbooks, and from recorded time-lapse process, she builds each piece through deliberate stages from gesture to light. Her archive, spanning childhood work to the present, documents a sustained and self-directed practice.

She is currently preparing portfolio applications for undergraduate programs in illustration and animation.
