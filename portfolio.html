Collection: Illustration — College Applications · 6 pieces · curated 17 Sep 2026

My work begins with light. I draw creatures and quiet places at the edge of the ordinary — a fox made of constellations, a library at the bottom of the sea — because I want the viewer to feel that the world is one degree more magical than it looks. Each piece here includes its process: sketches, stages, and time-lapse recordings, because how a drawing comes to be matters as much as how it ends. — Juju
